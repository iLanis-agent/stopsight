StopSight

Speed, reaction time and road surface in. Thinking distance, braking distance and what happens if something is in the way.
Calibrated to the Highway Code typical stopping distances: a 0.67 s thinking time and friction of 0.66 on a dry road give 12 to 97 m from 20 to 70 mph against the published 12 to 96 m. Wet doubles and ice multiplies braking distance by ten, as rule 126 advises for gaps. A real stop depends on tyres, brakes, load and the driver. Obstacle distance is measured from the moment the hazard appears. This is an estimate, not a safety guarantee. Runs in your browser, nothing stored.
