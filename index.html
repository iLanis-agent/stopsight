StopSight
Ten mph more is not ten percent more danger. Braking distance grows with the square of speed.
Open the app
Something steps out 40 m ahead and you react in 1.5 s. At 30 mph you stop with 6 m to spare. At 40 mph you hit it at 27 mph.

What it does

  • Thinking, braking and total distance in metres, feet and car lengths
  • Dry, wet and ice, plus uphill and downhill grade
  • Obstacle check: stop in time, or the speed you hit it at
  • Calibrated to the Highway Code typical stopping distances
Calibrated to the Highway Code "Typical stopping distances" (gov.uk). An estimate, not a safety guarantee. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
